Aspose.Slides is a set of libraries for creating, reading, editing and converting PowerPoint and OpenDocument presentations without Microsoft PowerPoint.

It is available for .NET, Java, Android, C++, Python, Node.js and PHP, and as plugins that export reports from JasperReports and Reporting Services to PowerPoint and convert presentations in SharePoint.
