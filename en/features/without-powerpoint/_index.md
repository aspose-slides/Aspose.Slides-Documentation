## **Overview**

Use Aspose.Slides in your app or on a server without installing Microsoft PowerPoint. The library handles the presentation files itself. Choose the edition for your programming language and follow its setup guide.

## **FAQ**

**What do I need to install?**

Install Aspose.Slides and the runtime dependencies and fonts listed in the setup guide for your edition.
