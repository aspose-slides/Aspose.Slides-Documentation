## **Overview**

Export a presentation as a PDF for distribution, printing, or review. The resulting document presents slides as fixed pages, so recipients can read it without editing the source deck. The edition-specific guides explain export options, page selection, and the handling of notes.

## **FAQ**

**Can I keep editing the original presentation?**

Yes. Export writes a separate PDF; retain the original presentation for further editing.
